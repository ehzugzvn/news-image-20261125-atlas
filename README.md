## What's on the page

- **Wednesday 25 November 2026** as the largest element, with a strip counting 38 stories across 7 regions.
- **Hero world map** with 14 numbered pins keyed to the day's stories.
- **4 leads**: Huawei guilty on 10 of 12 counts; Shenzhou-24 lifts off with a Pakistani pilot aboard; Russia's own figures show nearly 500,000 war dead; Huang apologises as Nvidia shares slide 5.9%.
- **Day in numbers** (10/12 · ≈500,000 · 47,000 · 11 · 5.9% · 1st) and a **UTC timeline** of the day.
- **10 tier-2 stories** with pictograms and verification tags, plus **briefs 15–38** grouped by region.
- **Date check** strip naming what was left out as other days' news, and a **How Sure** legend (C / O / R / F).

## How it runs

`render.py` draws `entry.png` (1024×1024) with PIL. `main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) copies `entry.png` into `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `/output`).
